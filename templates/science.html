Bridging Traditional Medicine and Modern Artificial Intelligence

InceptionV3 Architecture

LuminaLeaf XAI is built upon the InceptionV3 architecture, a state-of-the-art deep convolutional neural network developed by Google. Unlike standard networks, Inception uses "Inception Modules" that look at the image with multiple filter sizes (1x1, 3x3, 5x5) simultaneously.

For medicinal plants, this is critical. It allows the model to see both the fine details (leaf veins, serrated edges) and the broad context (leaf shape, stem structure) at the same time.

Grad-CAM: Why did it choose that?

One of the biggest problems in AI is the "Black Box" issue—not knowing why an AI made a prediction. We solve this using Grad-CAM (Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping).

When you upload an image, LuminaLeaf calculates the gradients of the target class flowing into the final convolutional layer. This creates a Heatmap that highlights the specific pixels that triggered the identification. This ensures transparency and builds trust in the results.
