🌿 LuminaLeaf XAI: Simplified Project Overview

WHAT IS IT?
LuminaLeaf is an AI-powered website that identifies medicinal plants from photos of their leaves. Think of it as a "Smart Botanical Assistant" that helps you discover the healing powers of nature.

HOW DOES IT WORK?
1. The AI "Brain": Uses a Google-developed neural network (InceptionV3) that is 48 layers deep. It has been specifically trained on a dataset of ~1,800 medicinal leaf images to recognize 30+ different species.
   - Training Accuracy: 94.30%
   - Validation Accuracy: 93.44% (Industry-standard performance)
2. The "Why" (Grad-CAM): Most AI is a mystery. Our app uses "Explainable AI" to show you a heatmap. This highlights exactly which parts of the leaf (veins, edges, texture) the AI used to make its decision.
3. Personal Dashboard: Users can sign up to save their scanning history and track their botanical discoveries over time.

KEY FEATURES:
- Instant Identification: Upload a photo, get a result in seconds.
- Medicinal Knowledge: Access scientific data, traditional uses, and safety warnings for every plant.
- Feedback Loop: Users can tell the app if it was right or wrong, creating a database for future model improvements.
- Premium Design: A modern, easy-to-use interface that works on both desktop and mobile.

TECHNICAL STACK (Simple Terms):
- Backend: Python & Flask (The logic)
- AI Engine: TensorFlow & Keras (The intelligence)
- Frontend: HTML, CSS, & Bootstrap (The look)
- Database: SQLite (The storage)
